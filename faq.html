Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions

How do I get started?

  • Tap the rower to start or stop.
  • Tap the gear to change stroke rate, drive ratio, direction, displayed stats, and colors.
  • In version 1.1, tap the grid to choose a character.
  • Use Siri to start, stop, or adjust stroke rate hands-free.

What’s free and what costs money?

Version 1.0 includes the Stick Figure, which is always free. Version 1.1 adds dozens of characters. The Stick Figure and two new rowing handles are Free. Every other character is free to try for 14 days from the first time you open version 1.1. After that, one small one-time purchase unlocks all of them. There are no subscriptions, no ads, and no pop-up reminders.

What happens when the 14-day trial ends?

This applies to version 1.1. The Free characters keep working as before. If you pick another character, RowBoRow offers to unlock all characters with the one-time purchase, or you can keep using any of the Free characters, such as the Stick Figure, at no cost.

How do I restore my purchase?

In version 1.1, choose any character that needs the unlock. On the screen that appears, tap Restore Purchases. Your purchase is tied to your Apple Account, so it also works on your other devices that use the same account.

How do I use RowBoRow with other apps?

On an iPad, RowBoRow can run in a small window beside apps like ErgData or ErgZone, so you can follow along while you row. To set this up, turn on Windowed Apps on your iPad (requires iPadOS 26 or later):

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPad.
  2. Tap Multitasking & Gestures in the left sidebar.
  3. Select Windowed Apps.

On earlier versions of iPadOS, you can use Split View or Slide Over to keep RowBoRow next to another app.

How do I use Siri with RowBoRow?

Just say something like “Hey Siri, start RowBoRow” or “Hey Siri, stop RowBoRow.” You can also ask Siri to adjust your stroke rate, for example “Hey Siri, set RowBoRow to 30.” Siri requests are handled by Apple under Apple’s own privacy practices. RowBoRow never receives your voice.

Will my screen stay on while I row?

Yes. RowBoRow keeps your screen awake while open so the display doesn’t dim in the middle of a workout.

Does RowBoRow make any sound?

No. RowBoRow is purely visual, so you can listen to an audiobook, music, or a podcast while you row.

Does it connect to my rowing machine?

No. RowBoRow works on its own and doesn’t connect to your rowing machine or any other device. You set the stroke rate and follow along.

Are my settings saved?

Yes. Your settings are saved on your device and are never sent anywhere.
