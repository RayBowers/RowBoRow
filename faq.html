Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions

What’s free and what costs money?

Figures in the Free category will always be free. These include the Stick Figure and two rowing handles.

Characters in the other categories are free to try for 14 days. After that, one small one-time purchase unlocks all of them. There are no subscriptions, no ads, no pop-up reminders and no loss of functionality.

What happens when the 14-day trial ends?

The Free characters keep working as before. If you pick another character, RowBoRow shows the in-app purchase screen to unlock all characters. If not intersted, just cancel out of it and you can keep using any of the Free characters, such as the Stick Figure, at no cost.

How do I restore my purchase?

Choose any character that needs the unlock. On the screen that appears, tap Restore Purchases. Your purchase is tied to your Apple or Google account, so it also works on your other devices that use the same account.

How do I use RowBoRow with other apps?

On an Android tablet, RowBoRow can run split screen beside workout apps like ErgData or ErgZone.

On an iPad, RowBoRow can run in a small window beside workout apps like ErgData or ErgZone, so you can follow along while you row. To set this up, turn on Windowed Apps on your iPad (requires iPadOS 26 or later):

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPad.
  2. Tap Multitasking & Gestures in the left sidebar.
  3. Select Windowed Apps.

On earlier versions of iPadOS, you can use Split View or Slide Over to keep RowBoRow next to another app.

What do the on-screen buttons do?

Under the stats are play/pause and stop buttons, and previous and next character buttons. Pause freezes the rower, and play picks up where you left off. Stop pauses and also resets the timer and stroke count. The previous and next buttons step through the characters in the order shown in the character grid, skipping any that are locked. To hide the buttons, turn off Display Buttons in settings.

How do I use Siri or Google with RowBoRow?

Just say something like “Hey Siri, start RowBoRow” or “Hey Google, start RowBoRow.” The same commands work with both:

  • “Set RowBoRow to 30” to change the strokes per minute
  • “Start RowBoRow” to start rowing
  • “Pause RowBoRow” to pause rowing
  • “Resume RowBoRow” to continue after a pause
  • “Stop RowBoRow” to stop rowing and reset the timer and stroke count
  • “Next rower in RowBoRow” or “previous” to change the rowing character

Siri requests are handled by Apple and Google requests are handled by Google, each under their own privacy practices. RowBoRow never receives your voice.

Will my screen stay on while I row?

Yes. RowBoRow keeps your screen awake while open so the display does not dim or go off in the middle of a workout.

Does RowBoRow make any sound?

No. RowBoRow is purely visual, so you can listen to an audiobook, music, or a podcast while you row.

I programmed RowBoRow this way because it is how I use it. I can follow along, often listening to an audiobook. However, if other people indicate they would also find audible feedback useful, let me know what you are looking for. I am open to adding more useful features.

Does RowBoRow connect to my rowing machine?

No. RowBoRow works on its own and does not connect to your rowing machine or any other device. You set the stroke rate and follow along.

RowBoRow was not meant to take the place of dedicated rowing or workout apps.

Are my settings saved?

Yes. Your settings are saved on your device and are never sent anywhere.
