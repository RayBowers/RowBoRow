RowBoRow

Visual metronome for rowers!

RowBoRow is a visual metronome for indoor rowers. Set your stroke rate, then row in time with the animation, so you can focus on your pace, an audiobook, a podcast or anything else during your workout.

  • Tap the rower to start or stop.
  • Tap the gear to change settings.
  • Use Siri to start, stop or adjust the stroke rate hands-free.
  • Works on iPhone and iPad, and on Mac as an iPad app.
  • Next Release – dozens of new characters and Apple TV support.

Settings

Tap the gear to make RowBoRow your own.

  • Stroke Rate – set your target strokes per minute
  • Drive Ratio – set how long the recovery takes compared to the drive (for example 1:2)
  • Flip Direction – mirror the figure to face left or right
  • Display Stats – show or hide elapsed time, stroke rate and stroke count
  • Foreground & Background – pick any colors you like, or tap Reset Colors to go back to the defaults

Use with ErgData and ErgZone

On an iPad, RowBoRow can run in a window beside other rowing apps such as ErgData or ErgZone, so you can follow along while you row (requires iPadOS 26 or later).

Privacy

✓ Data Not Collected

RowBoRow does not collect any data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your settings stay on your device and are never sent anywhere. It is simply an animated rower to follow along with.
