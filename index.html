RowBoRow

Set your stroke rate and follow along!

RowBoRow is a simple utility to help indoor rowers set a stroke rate and follow it without drifting. The visual provides an easy way to stay in sync so you can focus on your pace, an audio book or anything else while rowing along.

  • Tap the rower to start/stop
  • Edit settings under the gear
  • Use Siri to start/stop or adjust stroke rate hands-free!

Coming soon to the Apple App Store.

Use with ErgData and ErgZone

On an iPad, RowBoRow can be pulled up in a small window allowing you to follow along while still using other rowing apps such as ErgData or ErgZone.

Character Options -- UNDER DEVELOPMENT!

Pick from a variety of characters to row along with, from a classic stick figure to a pirate, a dragon or even a rowing T. rex!

Privacy

✓ Data Not Collected

RowBoRow does not collect any data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your settings stay on your device and are never sent anywhere. It is simply an animated rower to keep you on pace.
