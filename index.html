RowBoRow

Visual metronome for indoor rowers

RowBoRow is a visual metronome for indoor rowers. Set your stroke rate, then row in time with the animation, so you can focus on your pace, an audiobook, a podcast, or anything else during your workout.

  • Tap the rower to start or resume.
  • Tap the gear to change settings.
  • Tap the grid icon to switch characters.
  • Use Siri or Google voice commands for hands-free operation.
  • Works on iPhone, iPad, Mac (as an iPad app) and Apple TV.

Android version coming soon.

Why RowBoRow?

At work, I am a Senior Data Engineer, and spend all my time processing data. I recently wanted to have some fun at home making an app for my phone. Since I love rowing, this felt like a fun project to try.

My initial goal was to create a simple visual to row along with while listening to audio books, music, etc. Of course, after many hours following a Stick Figure I wanted to add some more fun, hence all the extra characters. My current favorites: Pirate and Alien!

Besides fun characters, I also added voice commands (Siri and Google) to change settings without stopping. Let me know if there are any other features you would like to see. ray@bowers.com.

Settings

Tap the gear to make RowBoRow your own.

  • Stroke Rate – set your target strokes per minute
  • Drive Ratio – set how long the drive takes compared to the recovery (for example 1 count drive, 2 counts recovery is 1:2). Ranges from 1:1.0 to 1:3.0 in 0.1 increments.
  • Flip Direction – mirror the figure to face left/top or right/down (except Gadgets... They always face the same way)
  • Display Time/SPM/Strokes – show or hide elapsed time, stroke rate, and stroke count
  • Display Play/Figure/SPM Buttons – show or hide the play/pause, stop, previous/next character, and decrease/increase SPM buttons
  • Foreground & Background – pick any colors you like, or tap Reset Colors to go back to the defaults

Use with ErgData and ErgZone

On a tablet, RowBoRow can run in a window (or split screen) beside other rowing apps such as ErgData or ErgZone, so you can follow along while you row.
