RowBoRow

Set your stroke rate and follow along!

RowBoRow is a simple utility to help indoor rowers set a stroke rate and follow it without drifting. The visual provides an easy way to stay in sync so you can focus on your pace, an audio book or anything else while rowing along.

  • Tap the rower to start/stop
  • Edit settings under the gear
  • Change characters via the grid - Coming Soon
  • Use Siri to start/stop or adjust stroke rate hands-free!

Coming soon to the Apple App Store.

Controls

Tap the gear to make RowBoRow your own.

  • Stroke Rate – set your target strokes per minute
  • Drive Ratio – adjust how long the recovery lasts compared to the drive
  • Facing – have the rower face left or right
  • Display Stats – show or hide elapsed time, stroke rate and stroke count
  • Foreground & Background – pick any colors you like, or tap Reset Colors to go back to the defaults

Character Options Coming soon

Pick from a variety of characters to row along with, from a classic stick figure to a pirate, a dragon or even a rowing T. rex! Prefer something simpler? Choose a meter like a battery, a thermometer or a steaming mug.

Use with ErgData and ErgZone

On an iPad, RowBoRow can run in a window allowing you to follow along while using other rowing apps such as ErgData or ErgZone.

Privacy

✓ Data Not Collected

RowBoRow does not collect any data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your settings stay on your device and are never sent anywhere. It is simply an animated rower to follow along with.
