Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

✓ Data Not Collected

RowBoRow does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data.

What I collect

Nothing. RowBoRow has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking of any kind, and it does not use third-party SDKs that collect data.

Settings on your device

Your preferences, such as stroke rate, drive ratio, colors, and chosen character, are saved only on your device. They are never sent to me or to anyone else.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you use Siri to start, stop, or adjust RowBoRow, the request is handled by Apple under Apple’s own privacy practices. RowBoRow does not receive or store your voice data.

Purchases and the free trial

RowBoRow offers a one-time purchase that unlocks additional characters. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. RowBoRow does not see or store your payment details, and it only asks Apple, on your device, whether the unlock has been purchased. The date your 14-day free trial started is saved only on your device.

Artificial intelligence

RowBoRow was developed with the assistance of AI coding tools. The app itself is not AI powered: it contains no AI features, and no user data is collected or sent to any AI service.

Website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts. If you email me, I use your message only to reply to you.

Children

Because RowBoRow collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Email ray@bowers.com.
