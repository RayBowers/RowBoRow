Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

RowBoRow does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. RowBoRow has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind, and it does not use third-party SDKs that collect data.

Settings on your device

Your preferences, such as stroke rate, drive ratio, colors, and chosen character, are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you use Siri to start, stop, or adjust RowBoRow, the request is handled by Apple on your device under Apple's own privacy practices. RowBoRow does not receive or store your voice data.

Website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts. If you email us, we use your message only to reply to you.

Children

Because RowBoRow collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
