Help for RowBoRow

Need help or have an idea? Email ray@bowers.com and I'll get back to you. I usually reply within a few days.

Requirements

RowBoRow runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 / iPadOS 17 or later. It also runs on Mac as an iPad app. Running it in a window beside other apps on iPad requires iPadOS 26 or later.

Reporting a problem

When you email, please include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are welcome.
