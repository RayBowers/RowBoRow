Help for RowBoRow

Need help or have an idea? Email ray@bowers.com and I'll get back to you. I usually reply within a few days.

Requirements

RowBoRow runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 / iPadOS 17 or later. Running it in a window beside other apps on iPad requires iPadOS 26 or later.

Getting started

  • Tap the rower to start or stop.
  • Tap the gear to change stroke rate, drive ratio, facing, displayed stats, and colors.
  • Tap the grid to change characters.
  • Use Siri to start, stop, or adjust stroke rate hands-free.

Using it with other rowing apps

On an iPad, RowBoRow can run in a small window beside apps like ErgData or ErgZone so you can follow along while you row. This requires iPadOS 26 or later. To do this, you need to Enable Windowed Apps on your iPad:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPad.
  2. Tap Multitasking & Gestures in the left sidebar.
  3. Select Windowed Apps.

On earlier versions of iPadOS, you can use Split View or Slide Over to keep RowBoRow next to another app.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use Siri with RowBoRow?

Just say something like “Hey Siri, start RowBoRow” or “Hey Siri, stop RowBoRow.” You can also ask Siri to adjust your stroke rate. Siri requests are handled by Apple on your device.

Will my screen stay on while I row?

Yes. RowBoRow keeps your screen awake while the rower is running so the display doesn’t dim in the middle of a workout.

Does RowBoRow make any sound?

No. RowBoRow is purely visual, so you can listen to an audiobook, music, or a podcast while you row.

Does it connect to my rowing machine?

No. RowBoRow works on its own and doesn’t connect to your rowing machine or any other device. You set the stroke rate and follow along.

Are my settings saved?

Yes. Your settings are saved on your device and are never sent anywhere.

Reporting a problem

When you email, please include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are welcome.

Privacy

RowBoRow collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
