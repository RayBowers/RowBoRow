Support

Help for RowBoRow

Need help or have an idea? Email ray@bowers.com and I’ll get back to you. I usually reply within a few days.

Requirements

Android: RowBoRow runs on phones and tablets with Android 12 (API level 31) or later. Voice commands on Android use Google.

iOS/iPadOS: RowBoRow runs on iPhones and iPads with iOS / iPadOS 17 or later. Running it in a window beside other apps on iPad requires iPadOS 26 or later. RowBoRow also runs on Mac as an iPad app.

tvOS: Apple TV runs on tvOS17 or later.

Reporting a problem

When you email, please include your device model, operating system version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are welcome.
